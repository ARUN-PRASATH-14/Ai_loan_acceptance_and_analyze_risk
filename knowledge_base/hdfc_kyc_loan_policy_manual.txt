CHAPTER 1: HDFC BANK DIGITAL KYC & CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD) POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Regulatory Alignment:
HDFC Bank enforces digital-first customer verification under RBI Master Directions 2025-2026, PMLA rules, and Central KYC Records Registry (CKYCR) standards. HDFC Bank is India's largest private sector bank and follows the most stringent digital onboarding standards.

1.2 Officially Valid Documents (OVDs) Accepted:
1. Proof of Identity & Address: Passport, Driving Licence, Voter ID Card, Proof of possession of Aadhaar (Masked format), NREGA Job Card.
2. Tax Identification: PAN Card (Mandatory for all credit facilities and accounts). Form 60 accepted for rural agri-borrowers only.
3. Recency of Utility Proof: Utility bill (Electricity/Gas/Telephone) not older than 60 days from date of application.

1.3 HDFC Instant Video KYC (V-CIP) Framework:
- 100% paperless onboarding for savings accounts, personal loans, and credit cards via HDFC Portal or Mobile App.
- Real-time facial recognition matching against PAN card and Aadhaar photo, geo-location validation within India, and live interaction with an authorized HDFC officer.
- Instant account opening with full transaction capability within 30 minutes.

1.4 Periodic KYC Updation Standards & RBI Relief (2025-2026):
- High Risk Accounts: Full KYC refresh every 2 years.
- Medium Risk Accounts: Full KYC refresh every 8 years.
- Low Risk Accounts: Full KYC refresh every 10 years (Low risk accounts due in 2025 extended to June 30, 2026 per RBI 2025-2026 directive).
- Digital re-KYC available via HDFC NetBanking or HDFC MobileBank app.

CHAPTER 2: HDFC GENERAL UNDERWRITING & CREDIT RISK NORMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Credit Score (CIBIL) Slabs & Pricing Grid:
- CIBIL Score 800 and above: Exceptional Prime Category. Lowest interest rate, instant pre-approved disbursal, zero processing fees.
- CIBIL Score 750 to 799: Prime Category. Lowest standard interest rate tier, instant pre-approved disbursal eligibility.
- CIBIL Score 700 to 749: Standard Category. Standard interest rate with normal processing fees.
- CIBIL Score 650 to 699: Acceptable with +0.25% to +0.50% interest markup. Requires Deputy Branch Head sanction.
- CIBIL Score 600 to 649: Requires +0.50% interest markup and Zonal Credit Head sanction. Available only for Salary Account holders.
- CIBIL Score below 600: Hard Rejection. No loan considered.
- New to Credit (NTC / Score 0 or -1): Allowed for Home Loans subject to strict FOIR cap of 50%.

2.2 Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR) Caps:
- Salaried Borrowers (CIBIL 650 and above): Maximum FOIR 60%.
- Self-Employed Borrowers: Maximum FOIR 55%.
- NTC (New To Credit) Borrowers: Strictly capped at 50% FOIR.
- HDFC Preferred / Imperia Salary Account Holders: Granted 5% FOIR relaxation (up to 65% max FOIR).
- Tier A MNC Employer with HDFC Salary Account: Additional 5% FOIR relaxation (up to 70% max FOIR with Zonal Head approval).

2.3 Income Multipliers for Tamil Nadu Cities:
- Chennai (Tier 1 Metro): Income thresholds 20% higher than base.
- Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem (Tier 2): Standard base income thresholds.
- Vellore, Rural TN (Tier 3): Income thresholds 20% lower than base (accessible for lower income brackets).

CHAPTER 3: HDFC HOME LOAN UNDERWRITING & ELIGIBILITY GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Target Profile & Age Limits:
- Applicant Category: Salaried, Self-Employed Professionals, Self-Employed Businessmen, NRIs.
- Minimum Entry Age: 21 years.
- Maximum Exit Age: 65 years at loan maturity (Salaried); 70 years at loan maturity (Self-Employed).

3.2 Income Requirements for Home Loans:
- Salaried (Tier 1 - Chennai): Minimum Net Monthly Income ₹30,000 per month.
- Salaried (Tier 2 - Coimbatore, Madurai): Minimum Net Monthly Income ₹25,000 per month.
- Salaried (Tier 3 - Vellore, Rural TN): Minimum Net Monthly Income ₹20,000 per month.
- Self-Employed: Minimum annual net profit ₹3,00,000 with 2 years audited financials.

3.3 Loan to Value (LTV) Ratios:
- Loan Amount up to ₹30,00,000: Maximum LTV 90% of property cost.
- Loan Amount ₹30,00,001 to ₹75,00,000: Maximum LTV 80%.
- Loan Amount above ₹75,00,000: Maximum LTV 75%.

3.4 Interest Rates for HDFC Home Loans (2025-2026):
- CIBIL 800+: From 8.50% p.a. (EBLR linked).
- CIBIL 750-799: From 8.55% p.a.
- CIBIL 700-749: From 8.65% p.a.
- CIBIL 650-699: From 8.90% p.a.
- NTC Borrowers: From 9.00% p.a. with strict 50% FOIR cap.
- HDFC Preferred Salary Account: Additional 0.05% concession.
- Processing Fee: 0.50% of loan amount or maximum ₹7,500 (waived for Preferred customers).

3.5 Maximum Tenure for Home Loans: 30 years (360 months).

CHAPTER 4: HDFC PERSONAL LOAN UNDERWRITING POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Income & Employment Vintage Requirements:
- Salaried Employees in Listed / Corporate Companies:
  a) Metro / Tier 1 (Chennai): Minimum Net Monthly Income (NMI) ₹25,000 per month.
  b) Tier 2 (Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem): Minimum NMI ₹22,000 per month.
  c) Tier 3 / Rural (Vellore, Rural TN): Minimum NMI ₹20,000 per month.
- Minimum Total Work Experience: 2 years, with at least 1 year in current employer.
- Minimum Self-Employed Business Vintage: 2 years of audited business operations.
- Maximum Loan Quantum: ₹40,00,000 (10-second disbursal for pre-approved HDFC salary account holders).
- Maximum Tenure: 60 months (5 years).

4.2 Interest Rates for HDFC Personal Loans:
- CIBIL 800+: From 10.25% p.a.
- CIBIL 750-799: From 10.75% p.a.
- CIBIL 700-749: From 11.50% p.a.
- CIBIL 650-699: From 12.50% p.a.
- Processing Fee: 2.50% of loan amount (waived for Preferred salary account holders).

4.3 HDFC Personal Loan - NTC (New to Credit) Borrowers:
- Minimum income ₹35,000 NMI per month for Tier 1; ₹28,000 for Tier 2.
- Maximum FOIR 40% for NTC Personal Loan customers.
- Maximum loan amount ₹2,00,000 for first-time NTC borrowers.

4.4 Prepayment & Foreclosure Charges:
- Zero foreclosure fee for personal loans after 12 EMIs for Preferred salary account holders.
- Standard borrowers: 3% pre-closure fee on outstanding principal.

CHAPTER 5: HDFC EDUCATION LOAN POLICY (STUDENT CREDIT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Institutional Approval & Loan Limits:
- Premier Institutes (India - IITs, IIMs, ISB, XLRI, AIIMS, NLUs): Up to ₹50,00,000 WITHOUT COLLATERAL.
- Recognized Indian Universities (NITs, premier state colleges): Up to ₹10,00,000 WITHOUT COLLATERAL; Above ₹10,00,000 requires tangible collateral.
- Studies Abroad (US, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany): Up to ₹1,50,00,000 with 100% tangible security.

5.2 Interest Rates for Education Loans:
- Up to ₹7.5 lakhs: From 10.00% p.a.
- Above ₹7.5 lakhs: From 10.50% p.a.
- Women Students: 0.50% concession on interest rate.
- Moratorium: Course duration + 12 months. No EMI during moratorium; interest capitalized.

5.3 Moratorium & Co-Borrower Terms:
- Mandatory Co-Borrower: Parent / Spouse with verifiable income.
- Maximum repayment period: 15 years after moratorium.
- CGFSEL (Credit Guarantee Fund for Education Loans): Available for eligible education loan amounts up to ₹7.5 lakhs.

CHAPTER 6: HDFC KISAN GOLD CARD (KGC) & AGRICULTURAL FINANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Scope & Scale of Finance:
- Crop loan limits fixed based on District Level Technical Committee (DLTC) Scale of Finance per acre.
- Investment Credit: For Tractors, Drip Irrigation, Dairy, Solar Pump sets (up to 7 years repayment).
- Collateral-Free Limit: Up to ₹1,60,000 standard limit; Up to ₹2,00,000 with digital land records (Tamil Nadu Chitta/Adangal).

6.2 Interest Subvention & Rates:
- Benchmark Crop Rate: 7.00% p.a. for loans up to ₹3,00,000.
- Government 2% Interest Subvention + 3% Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI) reduces effective rate to 4.00% p.a. for timely repaying farmers.

CHAPTER 7: HDFC MSME & BUSINESS LOANS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Business Growth Loans & MUDRA:
- Business Loans up to ₹75,00,000 collateral-free for self-employed professionals and business entities with 3 years vintage and positive net worth.
- MUDRA Loans: Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (₹50,001 to ₹5 lakhs), Tarun (₹5 to ₹10 lakhs), Tarun Plus (₹10 to ₹20 lakhs).
- CGTMSE Coverage: Collateral-free support up to ₹5,00,00,000.

7.2 Interest Rates for HDFC Business Loans:
- CIBIL 750+: From 10.00% p.a.
- CIBIL 700-749: From 11.00% p.a.
- CIBIL 650-699: From 12.50% p.a.

CHAPTER 8: CUSTOMER RIGHTS & DIGITAL LENDING CODE AT HDFC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 Key Fact Statement (KFS) & APR:
- Transparent KFS generated automatically for all digital applications with complete APR disclosure.

8.2 Grievance Escalation:
- Step 1: Branch Manager.

CHAPTER 9: EXHAUSTIVE DOCUMENTATION CHECKLIST FOR HDFC LOANS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Salaried (Home/Personal Loan): PAN, Aadhaar, 3 months salary slips, 6 months salary bank statement, Form 16.
- Self-Employed (Home/Business Loan): PAN, Aadhaar, 2 years ITR with P&L and Balance Sheet, GST 12 months filings, 12 months bank statements.
- Education Loan: PAN, Aadhaar, Admission Letter, Fee Schedule, Co-borrower income proof, Collateral documents if above ₹10 lakhs.
- KGC/Agri: PAN, Aadhaar, Chitta/Adangal land document, VAO certificate.

CHAPTER 10: HARD RULES & POLICY DEVIATIONS AT HDFC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 Hard Rejection Trigger Points (Strict, No Deviation):
- Applicant age below 21 or above 65 (Salaried) / 70 (Self-Employed) at loan maturity.
- CIBIL score below 600 (hard floor, no deviation).
- FOIR exceeding 60% (salaried) or exceeding 50% (NTC) without relationship leniency.
- 30+ Days Past Due (DPD) in last 12 months.
- Loan amount exceeding LTV limit.

10.2 Preferred Customer Concessions:
- HDFC Imperia / Preferred Salary Account customers: 50 points CIBIL relaxation (minimum 650 with Zonal approval), zero processing fees, priority disbursal.
- HDFC PayZapp or HDFC Credit Card holders with 2+ years relationship: 5% FOIR relaxation.
