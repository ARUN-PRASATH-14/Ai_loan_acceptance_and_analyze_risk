CHAPTER 1: CANARA BANK KYC & CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD) POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Regulatory Scope:
Canara Bank, one of India's largest public sector banks headquartered in Bengaluru, adheres strictly to RBI Master Directions 2025-2026 on KYC/AML/CFT, PMLA 2002, and Central KYC Records Registry (CKYCR) requirements. Canara Bank has a strong network in Tamil Nadu with branches across Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Salem, Trichy, and Vellore.

1.2 Officially Valid Documents (OVDs) Accepted:
1. Proof of Identity & Address: Passport, Driving Licence, Voter ID Card, Proof of possession of Aadhaar (Masked format), NREGA Job Card.
2. Tax Identification: PAN Card (Mandatory for credit facilities and account opening). Form 60 accepted for rural agri-borrowers only.
3. Recency of Utility Bills: Electricity/Piped Gas/Telephone bills within 60 days of application.

1.3 Canara Digital Onboarding & Video KYC (V-CIP):
- Customers can onboard digitally via Canara ai1 Mobile Banking App or official website.
- V-CIP includes live officer video call, facial matching against Aadhaar/PAN, Indian geo-location validation, and ITD database PAN verification.

1.4 Periodic KYC Updation Standards (2025-2026):
- High Risk Accounts: Every 2 years.
- Medium Risk Accounts: Every 8 years.
- Low Risk Accounts: Every 10 years (Low Risk accounts due in 2025 extended to June 30, 2026 per RBI circular).
- Digital re-KYC supported via Canara NetBanking and ai1 Mobile App.

CHAPTER 2: CANARA BANK GENERAL UNDERWRITING & CREDIT RISK MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Credit Score (CIBIL) Slabs & Accessible Baseline:
- Canara Bank has the most accessible CIBIL baseline among major Indian banks.
- CIBIL Score 800 and above: Exceptional. Lowest rate, zero processing fees.
- CIBIL Score 750 to 799: Preferred. Lowest standard interest rate tier and processing fee concessions.
- CIBIL Score 700 to 749: Good. Standard interest rate; standard fees.
- CIBIL Score 650 to 699: Standard Acceptable. Normal rates, priority sector access.
- CIBIL Score 600 to 649: Accessible with +0.25% to 0.50% markup; Canara's most differentiating threshold (competitors reject at this level).
- CIBIL Score below 600: Hard Rejection unless supported by liquid collateral (FD/NSC/LIC policy).
- NTC (New to Credit / CIBIL Score 0 or -1): Allowed for Home Loans and Personal Loans subject to income proof and strict FOIR of 50%.

2.2 Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR) Limits:
- Net Monthly Income up to ₹50,000: Maximum FOIR 50%.
- Net Monthly Income ₹50,001 to ₹1,00,000: Maximum FOIR 55%.
- Net Monthly Income above ₹1,00,000: Maximum FOIR 60%.
- Canara Bank Salary Package Account Holders: Additional 5% FOIR relaxation (up to 65% max).
- Tier A MNC Employees with Salary Account: Up to 70% FOIR with Circle Head approval.

2.3 Income Multipliers for Tamil Nadu Cities:
- Chennai (Tier 1 Metro): Income thresholds 20% higher than base.
- Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem (Tier 2): Standard base income thresholds.
- Vellore, Rural TN (Tier 3): Income thresholds 20% lower than base.

CHAPTER 3: CANARA BANK HOME LOAN UNDERWRITING GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Target Borrower Profile & Age Limits:
- Borrower Types: Salaried Individuals, Self-Employed Professionals, Agriculturists, NRIs.
- Minimum Entry Age: 21 years.
- Maximum Exit Age: 75 years at maturity of loan. This is the HIGHEST maximum age among all major Indian banks.

3.2 Income Requirements for Canara Home Loans:
- Salaried (Tier 1 - Chennai): Minimum Net Monthly Income ₹25,000 per month.
- Salaried (Tier 2 - Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem): Minimum Net Monthly Income ₹20,000 per month.
- Salaried (Tier 3 - Vellore, Rural TN): Minimum Net Monthly Income ₹15,000 per month.
- Self-Employed: Minimum net annual profit ₹2,40,000 (₹20,000 per month) for 2 years.
- Co-applicant income included in eligibility computation.

3.3 Loan to Value (LTV) Ratios:
- Loan Amount up to ₹30,00,000: Maximum LTV 90% of property cost.
- Loan Amount ₹30,00,001 to ₹75,00,000: Maximum LTV 80%.
- Loan Amount above ₹75,00,000: Maximum LTV 75%.

3.4 Interest Rates for Canara Home Loans (2025-2026):
- CIBIL 800+: From 8.35% p.a. (MCLR linked; among the lowest in public sector).
- CIBIL 750-799: From 8.45% p.a.
- CIBIL 700-749: From 8.55% p.a.
- CIBIL 650-699: From 8.70% p.a.
- CIBIL 600-649: From 9.00% p.a. (accessible threshold unique to Canara Bank).
- Women Borrowers: 0.05% concession on all home loan interest rates.
- Processing Fee: 0.50% of loan amount, maximum ₹15,000.

3.5 Maximum Tenure for Canara Home Loans: 30 years (360 months).

CHAPTER 4: CANARA BANK PERSONAL LOAN POLICY (CANARA BUDGET SCHEME)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Eligibility & Income Thresholds:
- Salaried Employees of Govt, PSUs, Reputed Public/Private Companies (ideally maintaining Salary Account with Canara Bank).
- Minimum Net Monthly Income (NMI):
  a) Chennai / Tier 1: Minimum NMI ₹25,000 per month.
  b) Tier 2 (Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem): Minimum NMI ₹20,000 per month.
  c) Tier 3 / Rural (Vellore, Rural TN): Minimum NMI ₹15,000 per month.
- Minimum Employment Vintage: 2 years total experience (minimum 1 year with current employer).
- Maximum Quantum: Up to 25 months gross salary, capped at ₹30,00,000.
- Maximum Tenure: 60 months (5 years).

4.2 Interest Rates for Canara Personal Loans:
- CIBIL 800+: From 10.00% p.a.
- CIBIL 750-799: From 10.50% p.a.
- CIBIL 700-749: From 11.25% p.a.
- CIBIL 650-699: From 12.00% p.a.
- CIBIL 600-649: From 13.00% p.a. (accessible to Canara salary account holders).
- Processing Fee: 1% of loan amount.

4.3 NTC (New to Credit) Borrowers:
- Minimum income ₹20,000 NMI for Tier 1; ₹16,000 for Tier 2; ₹12,000 for Tier 3.
- Maximum loan ₹1,00,000 for first NTC personal loan applicants.
- Maximum FOIR 40% for NTC applicants.

4.4 Canara Salary Account Concessions:
- Canara Salary Account holders: 25 basis points (0.25%) interest rate concession.
- Zero processing fees for salary account customers.
- Instant pre-approved personal loan up to ₹5,00,000 for qualifying salary customers.

CHAPTER 5: CANARA BANK EDUCATION LOAN POLICY (CANARA VIDYA TURANT & HIGHER EDUCATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Institutional Tiering & Loan Limits:
- Canara Vidya Turant (Premier Institutes - IITs, IIMs, NITs, IISc, AIIMS, NLUs): Up to ₹40,00,000 WITHOUT COLLATERAL.
- Standard Indian Higher Education (Recognized Universities): Up to ₹7,50,000 WITHOUT COLLATERAL; Above ₹7,50,000 requires tangible collateral or CGFSEL coverage.

- Vocational/Skill Development: Up to ₹1,50,000 under special schemes.

5.2 Interest Rates for Education Loans:
- Up to ₹7.5 lakhs: From 9.50% p.a.
- Above ₹7.5 lakhs: From 10.00% p.a.
- Women Students: 0.50% concession on interest rate.
- Moratorium: Course Duration + 12 months. No EMI during moratorium period.

5.3 Repayment & Subsidy Terms:
- Moratorium Period: Course Duration + 12 months.
- Maximum repayment period: 15 years post-moratorium.
- Central Sector Interest Subsidy (CSIS) available for eligible EWS students (family income up to ₹4,50,000 per year).
- CGFSEL guarantee available for loans up to ₹10 lakhs.

CHAPTER 6: CANARA KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC) & AGRICULTURAL CREDIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Target Farmers & Coverage:
- Owner Cultivators, Tenant Farmers, Oral Lessees, Sharecroppers, and Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) in Tamil Nadu and South India.
- Scale of Finance (SOF) approved by District Level Technical Committee (DLTC).
- Collateral-Free Limit: Up to ₹1,60,000 standard; Up to ₹2,00,000 where land records are verified digitally (Tamil Nadu e-Revenue / Chitta).

6.2 Subvention Interest Structure:
- Base Crop Loan Interest Rate: 7.00% p.a. for limits up to ₹3,00,000.
- Government 2% Interest Subvention + 3% Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI) reduces effective rate to 4.00% p.a. for prompt repayment.

6.3 KCC Documentation:
- PAN or Form 60, Aadhaar, Chitta/Adangal land document, VAO certificate, Crop cultivation record.

CHAPTER 10: HARD RULES & EXCEPTION HANDLING AT CANARA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 Hard Rejection Trigger Points (No Deviation):
- Age below 21 or above 75 at Home Loan maturity (highest age limit among Indian banks).
- CIBIL score below 600 (hard floor; below this only secured loans with liquid collateral).
- Employment vintage under 2 years for Salaried Personal Loan applicants.
- FOIR exceeding 60% without relationship leniency.
- LTV exceeding 90% (up to ₹30 lakhs), 80% (₹30-75 lakhs), or 75% (above ₹75 lakhs).

10.2 Leniency & Concessions (Canara's Unique Advantages):
- Canara Bank Salary Package account holders: 25 bps (0.25%) interest concession, 5% FOIR relaxation (up to 65%), zero processing fees.
- CIBIL 600-649 customers: Eligible for loans at higher rate without automatic rejection (unique among Indian banks).
- Priority Sector borrowers (Agriculture, Women, SC/ST): Enhanced credit access with lower income thresholds and relaxed CIBIL floors.
- Canara Bank is the preferred choice for senior borrowers (age 70-75) and lower CIBIL score (600-649) applicants.
