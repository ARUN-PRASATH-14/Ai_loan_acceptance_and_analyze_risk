CHAPTER 1: ICICI BANK KYC & DIGITAL CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD) POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Regulatory Scope:
ICICI Bank, India's second largest private sector bank, operates customer verification under RBI Master Directions 2025-2026, PMLA guidelines, and Central KYC Records Registry (CKYCR) integration.

1.2 Officially Valid Documents (OVDs):
1. Proof of Identity & Address: Passport, Driving Licence, Voter's Identity Card, Proof of possession of Aadhaar (Masked format), NREGA Job Card.
2. Tax Identifier: PAN Card mandatory for all banking and credit transactions. Form 60 accepted strictly for rural agricultural customers.
3. Proof of Address Recency: Utility bills (Electricity/Piped Gas) within 60 days of application.

1.3 Video Customer Identification Process (V-CIP):
- Paperless onboarding via ICICI iMobile Pay app.
- Automated OCR of PAN card, facial liveness check, geo-tagging (Indian geo-fencing), and live interaction with ICICI verification desk officer.
- Instant savings account opening and pre-approved loan access for eligible customers.

1.4 Periodic KYC Updation Standards (2025-2026):
- High Risk Accounts: Every 2 years.
- Medium Risk Accounts: Every 8 years.
- Low Risk Accounts: Every 10 years (Low Risk accounts due in 2025 extended to June 30, 2026 per RBI circular).
- Digital re-KYC supported via iMobile Pay and ICICI Internet Banking.

CHAPTER 2: ICICI GENERAL UNDERWRITING & CREDIT RISK NORMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Credit Score (CIBIL) & Risk Thresholds:
- CIBIL Score 800 and above: Exceptional. Instant pre-approved loans, lowest interest tier, zero processing fees.
- CIBIL Score 750 to 799: Prime. Lowest standard interest rate; instant disbursal for eligible products.
- CIBIL Score 700 to 749: Standard. Normal interest rate; standard processing fees.
- CIBIL Score 650 to 699: Eligible with +0.25% rate markup and Senior Credit Manager approval.
- CIBIL Score 600 to 649: Requires Senior Credit Manager approval and +0.25% to +0.50% rate markup. Available for salary account holders only.
- CIBIL Score below 650: Hard Rejection for Personal Loans. Minimum 650 is the floor for personal credit.
- CRITICAL HARD RULE: ICICI Bank strictly DOES NOT ALLOW New-to-Credit (NTC / CIBIL Score 0 or -1) applicants for Home Loans. All Home Loan applicants must have an established credit history.

2.2 Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR) Limits:
- Monthly Income up to ₹50,000: Maximum FOIR 50%.
- Monthly Income ₹50,001 to ₹1,00,000: Maximum FOIR 55%.
- Monthly Income above ₹1,00,000: Maximum FOIR 60%.
- ICICI Wealth/Salary Account Relationship: 5% additional FOIR relaxation for Tier A corporate employees.

2.3 Income Multipliers for Tamil Nadu Cities:
- Chennai (Tier 1 Metro): Income thresholds 20% higher than base.
- Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem (Tier 2): Standard base income thresholds.
- Vellore, Rural TN (Tier 3): Income thresholds 20% lower than base.

CHAPTER 3: ICICI HOME LOAN UNDERWRITING & RISK POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Target Borrower Profile & Age Bracket:
- Borrower Types: Resident Salaried, Self-Employed Professionals, NRIs.
- Minimum Entry Age: 20 years.
- Maximum Exit Age: 65 years at maturity of loan (Salaried); 70 years (Self-Employed).

3.2 Income Requirements for ICICI Home Loans:
- Salaried (Tier 1 - Chennai): Minimum Net Monthly Income ₹30,000 per month.
- Salaried (Tier 2 - Coimbatore, Madurai): Minimum Net Monthly Income ₹25,000 per month.
- Salaried (Tier 3 - Vellore): Minimum Net Monthly Income ₹20,000 per month.
- Self-Employed: Minimum net annual profit ₹3,00,000 for 2 consecutive years.

3.3 Loan to Value (LTV) Ratios:
- Loan Amount up to ₹30,00,000: Maximum LTV 85% of property market value.
- Loan Amount ₹30,00,001 to ₹75,00,000: Maximum LTV 80%.
- Loan Amount above ₹75,00,000: Maximum LTV 75%.

3.4 Interest Rates for ICICI Home Loans (2025-2026):
- CIBIL 800+: From 8.45% p.a. (EBLR linked).
- CIBIL 750-799: From 8.50% p.a.
- CIBIL 700-749: From 8.65% p.a.
- CIBIL 650-699: From 8.90% p.a.
- Processing Fee: 0.50% of loan amount or ₹3,000 minimum, maximum ₹10,000.
- NTC Borrowers: Not eligible for Home Loans at ICICI Bank.

3.5 Maximum Tenure for ICICI Home Loans: 30 years (360 months).

CHAPTER 4: ICICI PERSONAL LOAN UNDERWRITING POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Income & Employment Requirements:
- Salaried Employees:
  a) Chennai / Tier 1 Metro: Minimum Net Monthly Income (NMI) ₹25,000 per month.
  b) Tier 2 (Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem): Minimum Net Monthly Income ₹22,000 per month.
  c) Tier 3 / Rural (Vellore, Rural TN): Minimum Net Monthly Income ₹20,000 per month.
- Minimum Employment Vintage: 1 year continuous employment (minimum 6 months with current employer).
- Maximum Loan Quantum: Up to ₹50,00,000 (Instant disbursal via iMobile Pay for pre-approved customers).
- Maximum Tenure: 72 months (6 years).

4.2 Interest Rates for ICICI Personal Loans:
- CIBIL 800+: From 10.25% p.a.
- CIBIL 750-799: From 10.75% p.a.
- CIBIL 700-749: From 11.50% p.a.
- CIBIL 650-699: From 12.75% p.a.
- Processing Fee: 2.50% of loan amount (waived for Wealth salary package customers).

4.3 ICICI Personal Loan - NTC Borrowers:
- Minimum income ₹35,000 NMI for Tier 1; ₹28,000 for Tier 2.
- Maximum loan ₹2,00,000 for first NTC personal loan.
- Maximum FOIR 40% for NTC Personal Loan applicants.

4.4 Prepayment Rules:
- Pre-closure allowed after 12 EMIs with 3% fee for standard borrowers.
- Zero pre-closure fee for Wealth salary package clients.

CHAPTER 5: ICICI EDUCATION LOAN POLICY (STUDENT FINANCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Course & Institute Coverage:
- Premier Tier 1 Institutes (IITs, IIMs, ISB, AIIMS, NLUs): Up to ₹40,00,000 WITHOUT COLLATERAL.
- Tier 2 Indian Universities (NITs, premier state colleges): Up to ₹10,00,000 WITHOUT COLLATERAL; Above ₹10,00,000 requires 100% tangible security.
- Overseas Education (US, UK, Canada, Europe, Australia): Up to ₹1,00,00,000 with collateral security.

5.2 Interest Rates for Education Loans:
- Up to ₹7.5 lakhs: From 9.75% p.a.
- Above ₹7.5 lakhs: From 10.25% p.a.
- Women Students: 0.50% interest concession.
- Moratorium: Course duration + 12 months. No EMI during moratorium.

5.3 Moratorium & Co-Applicant Terms:
- Mandatory Co-Applicant: Parent / Legal Guardian with verifiable income source.
- Moratorium Period: Course duration + 12 months.
- Maximum repayment: 15 years post-moratorium.

CHAPTER 6: ICICI KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC) & AGRI FINANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Target Farmers & Eligibility:
- Owner Cultivators, Tenant Farmers, Sharecroppers, and Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) with valid land records.

6.2 Scale of Finance & Quantum of Credit:
- Determined based on District Level Technical Committee (DLTC) crop scale of finance multiplied by acreage.
- Collateral-Free Limit: Up to ₹1,60,000 for standard applicants; Up to ₹2,00,000 where land records are verified digitally (Tamil Nadu e-Revenue / Chitta).

6.3 Interest Rate & Subvention Structure:
- Base Crop Loan Interest: 7.00% p.a. for limits up to ₹3,00,000.
- Government 2% Interest Subvention + 3% Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI) yields an effective rate of 4.00% p.a.

CHAPTER 10: HARD REJECTION RULES & EXCEPTION HANDLING AT ICICI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 Hard Rejection Trigger Points (No Deviation):
- NTC applicant applying for Home Loan (Strict Rejection - no exception).
- Age below 20 or above 65 (Salaried) / 70 (Self-Employed) at loan maturity.
- CIBIL score below 650 for Personal Loans.
- FOIR exceeding 60% without relationship leniency.
- LTV exceeding 85% for Home Loans (up to ₹30 lakhs) or 80% (₹30-75 lakhs) or 75% (above ₹75 lakhs).

10.2 Relationship Leniency Framework:
- ICICI Wealth / Salary Account Relationship: 50 points CIBIL margin leniency (down to 600 CIBIL with credit committee sanction) and 5% FOIR relaxation for Tier A corporate employees.
- ICICI Privilege Banking Customers: Dedicated Relationship Manager support and priority underwriting turnaround of 2 working days.
