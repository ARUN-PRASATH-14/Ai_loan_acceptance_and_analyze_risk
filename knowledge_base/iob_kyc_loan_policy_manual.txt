CHAPTER 1: IOB KYC & CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD) POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Regulatory Scope:
Indian Overseas Bank (IOB), a premier public sector bank headquartered in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, strictly implements RBI Master Directions 2025-2026, PMLA 2002 rules, and CKYCR protocols across all branches and digital channels. IOB has a strong presence in Tamil Nadu with over 1,000 branches.

1.2 Officially Valid Documents (OVDs) Accepted:
1. Proof of Identity & Address: Passport, Driving Licence, Voter's Identity Card, Aadhaar Card (Masked e-KYC), NREGA Job Card.
2. Tax Identification: PAN Card (Mandatory). Form 60 accepted for small agricultural and rural borrowers.
3. Utility Proof: Electricity bill, Water bill, or Landline bill not older than 2 months.

1.3 IOB Digital KYC & Video CIP (V-CIP):
- Customers can onboard digitally through the IOB Mobile Banking App (IOB Mobile) or official web portal.
- V-CIP includes live officer video interaction, geo-location validation (within India), liveness verification, and PAN validation against ITD database.

1.4 Periodic KYC Updation Standards (2025-2026):
- High Risk Accounts: Every 2 years.
- Medium Risk Accounts: Every 8 years.
- Low Risk Accounts: Every 10 years (Low Risk accounts due in 2025 extended to June 30, 2026 per RBI circular).
- Digital re-KYC supported via IOB NetBanking and IOB Mobile app.

CHAPTER 2: IOB GENERAL UNDERWRITING & CREDIT RISK MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Credit Score (CIBIL) Thresholds:
- CIBIL Score 800 and above: Exceptional. Lowest interest rate, priority processing.
- CIBIL Score 750 to 799: Prime. Standard best rate.
- CIBIL Score 700 to 749: Good. Standard interest rate with normal processing.
- CIBIL Score 650 to 699: Acceptable baseline for standard processing. Standard rates apply.
- CIBIL Score 600 to 649: Requires Regional Manager recommendation and +0.25% interest rate spread.
- CIBIL Score below 600: Hard Rejection unless secured by liquid collateral (FD/NSC/KVP).
- NTC (New to Credit / Score 0 or -1): Allowed for Personal Loans with income proof; not for Home Loans without co-applicant.

2.2 Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR) Caps:
- Net Monthly Income up to ₹50,000: Maximum FOIR 50%.
- Net Monthly Income ₹50,001 to ₹1,00,000: Maximum FOIR 55%.
- Net Monthly Income above ₹1,00,000: Maximum FOIR 60%.
- Existing IOB Salary Account Holders: Additional 5% FOIR relaxation.

2.3 Income Multipliers for Tamil Nadu Cities:
- Chennai (Tier 1 Metro): Income thresholds 20% higher than base.
- Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem (Tier 2): Standard base income thresholds.
- Vellore, Rural TN (Tier 3): Income thresholds 20% lower than base.

CHAPTER 3: IOB HOME LOAN (SUBHAGRUHA) UNDERWRITING POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Target Borrower & Age Thresholds:
- Borrower Category: Salaried Employees, Self-Employed Professionals, Businessmen, NRIs.
- Minimum Entry Age: 21 years.
- Maximum Exit Age: 70 years at maturity of loan.

3.2 Income & CIBIL Requirements for IOB Home Loans:
- Minimum Gross Annual Income:
  a) Chennai / Metro (Tier 1): Minimum Gross Annual Income ₹6,00,000 per year (₹50,000 per month gross).
  b) Tier 2 (Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem): Minimum Gross Annual Income ₹5,00,000 per year (₹41,667 per month gross).
  c) Tier 3 / Rural (Vellore, Rural TN): Minimum Gross Annual Income ₹4,00,000 per year (₹33,333 per month gross).
- Minimum CIBIL Score: 650 for standard applicants (600 with Regional Manager sanction for existing salary account holders).
- Co-applicant income can be included for income eligibility computation.

3.3 Loan to Value (LTV) Ratios:
- Loan Amount up to ₹30,00,000: Maximum LTV 90%.
- Loan Amount ₹30,00,001 to ₹75,00,000: Maximum LTV 80%.
- Loan Amount above ₹75,00,000: Maximum LTV 75%.

3.4 Interest Rates for IOB Subhagruha Home Loans (2025-2026):
- CIBIL 800+: From 8.40% p.a. (MCLR linked).
- CIBIL 750-799: From 8.50% p.a.
- CIBIL 700-749: From 8.60% p.a.
- CIBIL 650-699: From 8.75% p.a.
- CIBIL 600-649 (with RM sanction): From 9.00% p.a.
- Processing Fee: 0.50% of loan amount, maximum ₹25,000.
- Women Borrowers: 0.05% concession on interest rate.

3.5 Maximum Tenure for IOB Home Loans: 30 years (360 months).

CHAPTER 4: IOB PERSONAL LOAN POLICY (ROYAL & CLEAN PERSONAL LOAN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Eligibility & Categorization:
- Category A (Lowest risk, best rate): Permanent employees of Central/State Govt, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), Defense personnel with IOB salary account.
- Category B (Standard): Permanent employees of reputed listed private MNCs and educational institutions.
- Category C (Higher risk): Self-employed professionals (Doctors, CAs, Engineers with practice proof).
- Minimum Employment Vintage: 2 years continuous service (minimum 1 year in current organization).

4.2 Income Requirements for IOB Personal Loans:
- Category A (Govt/PSU employees): Minimum NMI ₹15,000 (Tier 3), ₹20,000 (Tier 2), ₹25,000 (Tier 1 - Chennai).
- Category B (Private company employees): Minimum NMI ₹20,000 (Tier 3), ₹25,000 (Tier 2), ₹30,000 (Tier 1 - Chennai).
- Category C (Self-employed): Minimum net annual income ₹3,00,000.
- Maximum Quantum: Up to 20 times net monthly salary, capped at ₹25,00,000.
- Maximum Tenure: 60 months (5 years).

4.3 Interest Rates for IOB Personal Loans:
- Category A (Govt/PSU): From 11.00% p.a.
- Category B (Private): From 11.75% p.a.
- Category C (Self-employed): From 12.50% p.a.
- Processing Fee: 1% of loan amount.

CHAPTER 5: IOB EDUCATION LOAN POLICY (VIDYA JYOTI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Scheme Details & Limits:
- Premier Institutes in India (IITs, IIMs, NLUs, AIIMS, NITs): Up to ₹40,00,000 WITHOUT COLLATERAL.
- Approved Institutions in India: Up to ₹7,50,000 WITHOUT COLLATERAL; Above ₹7,50,000 requires 100% tangible security or CGFSEL coverage.
- Studies Abroad: Up to ₹1,50,00,000 with tangible collateral (residential land/building/FD).

5.2 Interest Rates for IOB Education Loans:
- Up to ₹7.5 lakhs: From 9.50% p.a.
- Above ₹7.5 lakhs: From 10.00% p.a.
- Women Students: 0.50% concession.
- Moratorium: Course duration + 12 months. Simple interest during moratorium; EMI begins after moratorium.

5.3 Moratorium & Interest Subsidy:
- Co-Borrower Requirement: Joint execution with parents/spouse mandatory.
- Moratorium Period: Course duration + 12 months.
- Central Sector Interest Subsidy (CSIS) available for EWS students (family income up to ₹4,50,000 per year).
- Maximum repayment: 15 years after moratorium.

CHAPTER 6: IOB KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC) & PRIORITY SECTOR AGRI FINANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Scope & Scale of Finance:
- IOB is a traditional leader in South Indian agricultural lending with strong presence in Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, and Karnataka.
- KCC provided to owner cultivators, tenant farmers, oral lessees, and Joint Liability Groups (JLGs).
- Scale of Finance per acre approved by District Level Technical Committee (DLTC).
- Collateral-Free Limit: Mandatory up to ₹1,60,000; Extended up to ₹2,00,000 for Tamil Nadu farmers with digitized Chitta/Adangal land verification.

6.2 Subvention Interest Rate (2025-2026):
- Benchmark Interest Rate: 7.00% p.a. for short-term crop loans up to ₹3,00,000.
- Government 2% Interest Subvention + 3% Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI) lowers effective rate to 4.00% p.a. for timely paying farmers.
- ATM-enabled RuPay KCC card for withdrawal at any ATM.

CHAPTER 7: IOB MSME & MUDRA LOANS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 IOB MSME Schemes:
- MUDRA Loans: Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (₹50,001 to ₹5 lakhs), Tarun (₹5 lakhs to ₹10 lakhs), Tarun Plus (₹10 lakhs to ₹20 lakhs).
- IOB SME Suvidha: Business loans up to ₹50,00,000 for entities with 3 years vintage.
- CGTMSE Coverage: Supported up to ₹5,00,00,000 for eligible MSME enterprises.

7.2 Interest Rates for Business Loans:
- CIBIL 750+: From 9.50% p.a.
- CIBIL 700-749: From 10.50% p.a.
- CIBIL 650-699: From 11.50% p.a.

CHAPTER 8: CUSTOMER RIGHTS & GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL AT IOB
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 Key Fact Statement & APR:
- Standardized KFS provided for all loan sanctions with complete APR disclosure.
- Look-up (Cooling-off) period: 3 days for personal loans, 7 days for housing loans.

8.2 Complaint Escalation:
- Step 1: Branch Manager.
- Step 2: IOB Customer Care 1800 425 4445 (toll-free).
- Step 3: Central Office Secretariat Chennai.
- Step 4: RBI Integrated Ombudsman (https://cms.rbi.org.in).

CHAPTER 9: EXHAUSTIVE DOCUMENTATION CHECKLIST FOR IOB LOANS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Salaried (Home/Personal Loan): PAN, Aadhaar, 3 months salary slips, 6 months salary account statement, Form 16.
- Self-Employed (Any Loan): PAN, Aadhaar, 3 years ITR, P&L statement, 12 months bank statements, GST returns.
- Education Loan: PAN, Aadhaar, Admission Letter, Fee Schedule, Co-borrower income proof, Collateral documents if above ₹7.5 lakhs.
- KCC/Agri: PAN or Form 60, Aadhaar, Chitta/Adangal land document, VAO certificate.

CHAPTER 10: HARD RULES & POLICY EXCEPTIONS AT IOB
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 Hard Rejection Criteria (No Deviation):
- Age below 21 or above 70 at Home Loan maturity.
- CIBIL score below 650 (hard floor without RM sanction; 600 with RM sanction).
- Annual Income below ₹6,00,000 for Metro Home Loans without co-applicant.
- FOIR exceeding 60% without relationship leniency.
- LTV exceeding 90% (up to ₹30 lakhs), 80% (₹30-75 lakhs), or 75% (above ₹75 lakhs).

10.2 Leniency Framework:
- Existing IOB Salary Account holders: 50 points CIBIL margin leniency (down to 600 CIBIL) and 5% FOIR relaxation with Regional Head approval.
- Women Borrowers: 0.05% interest concession on Home and Education loans.
- Priority Sector (Agriculture, Education, Women, SC/ST): Enhanced credit access with lower income thresholds.
