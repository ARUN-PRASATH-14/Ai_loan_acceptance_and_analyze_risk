CHAPTER 1: SBI KYC & CUSTOMER ONBOARDING STANDARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Mandate & Scope:
State Bank of India (SBI) enforces strict adherence to RBI Master Directions on KYC/AML/CFT. Every individual and legal entity opening an account or seeking credit facilities must undergo mandatory verification prior to account activation, credit sanction, or loan disbursal.

1.2 Officially Valid Documents (OVDs) Accepted at SBI:
1. Proof of Identity (PoI): PAN Card (Mandatory under IT Rule 114B), Passport, Voter ID Card, Aadhaar Card (Masked format), Driving Licence, NREGA Job Card signed by State Govt official.
2. Proof of Address (PoA): Aadhaar Card, Passport, Driving Licence, Voter ID, Utility Bills (Electricity/Piped Gas/Telephone bill not older than 2 months), Property or Municipal Tax Receipt.
3. Form 60: Allowed strictly for agricultural borrowers or individuals exempt from income tax, subject to verification by the Branch Manager.

1.3 Digital Onboarding via SBI YONO & Video KYC (V-CIP):
- Customers applying for digital savings accounts or express personal loans can complete Video-CIP on the SBI YONO application.
- Verification includes real-time face matching against Aadhaar/PAN photo, geotagging (ensuring physical presence within Indian borders), live PAN extraction, and OTP-based Aadhaar e-KYC.
- Digital accounts opened via V-CIP are treated on par with branch-opened accounts without operational restrictions.

1.4 Periodic KYC Updation Standards & RBI Relief Window (2025-2026):
- High Risk Customers: Full physical KYC updation required every 2 years.
- Medium Risk Customers: Full KYC updation required every 8 years.
- Low Risk Customers: Full KYC updation required every 10 years.
- RBI 2025-2026 Relief Window: Low-Risk individual accounts due for periodic updation in 2025 have until June 30, 2026 (or 1 year from original due date, whichever is later) to complete re-KYC. Self-declaration of no-change in address/KYC can be submitted through YONO App, SBI NetBanking, or ATM kiosks without visiting a branch.

CHAPTER 2: SBI GENERAL CREDIT UNDERWRITING & RISK EVALUATION MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Credit Score (CIBIL) Slabs & Pricing Grid:
- CIBIL Score 800 and above: Exceptional. Lowest MCLR rate, zero processing fee, instant digital disbursal.
- CIBIL Score 750 to 799: Prime Borrower. Eligible for benchmark MCLR/EBLR rate with zero spread. Zero processing fee during festive offers.
- CIBIL Score 700 to 749: Standard Category. Standard interest rate. Normal processing fees apply.
- CIBIL Score 650 to 699: Acceptable with Risk Markup of +0.25% to +0.50% spread. Subject to manual approval by Credit Processing Cell (CPC).
- CIBIL Score below 650: Automatic hard rejection for non-salary account holders. SBI Corporate Salary Package (CSP) customers enjoy 50 points relaxation, minimum floor is 650 CIBIL.
- NTC (New to Credit / CIBIL Score 0 or -1): Permitted for Home Loans with strict FOIR of 50%. Not permitted for standard unsecured Personal Loans unless income is above ₹30,000 per month.

2.2 Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR) Formula & Limits:
- Net Monthly Income (NMI) = Gross Monthly Income minus mandatory statutory deductions (PF, Income Tax, Professional Tax).
- FOIR Formula: (Total Existing Monthly EMIs + Proposed SBI Loan EMI) / NMI.
- NMI up to ₹50,000: Maximum FOIR 50%.
- NMI ₹50,001 to ₹1,00,000: Maximum FOIR 55%.
- NMI ₹1,00,001 to ₹2,00,000: Maximum FOIR 60%.
- NMI above ₹2,00,000: Maximum FOIR 65%.
- Existing SBI Salary Account Holders (CSP): 5% additional FOIR relaxation. Tier A MNC Employees: additional 5% FOIR relaxation.
- Applicants with Tier A (Top MNC) employer and salary account: FOIR up to 75% considered on a case-by-case basis by CPC.

2.3 Income Multipliers for Tamil Nadu Cities:
- Chennai (Tier 1 Metro): Income thresholds multiplied by 1.2x (20% higher standard).
- Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem (Tier 2): Standard multiplier 1.0x.
- Vellore, Rural TN (Tier 3): Income thresholds multiplied by 0.8x (20% lower threshold allowed).

CHAPTER 3: SBI HOME LOAN PRODUCTS (REGULAR, MAXGAIN, CRE, TRIBAL PLUS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Target Profile & Age Thresholds:
- Applicant Categories: Resident Salaried, Self-Employed Professionals, Self-Employed Businessmen, NRIs, PIOs, Agriculturists.
- Minimum Entry Age: 18 years.
- Maximum Exit Age: 70 years at loan maturity (age at sanction + tenure must not exceed 70 years).

3.2 Loan to Value (LTV) Ratios for Home Loans:
- Property Value up to ₹30,00,000: Maximum LTV 90% of property value.
- Property Value ₹30,00,001 to ₹75,00,000: Maximum LTV 80% of property value.
- Property Value above ₹75,00,000: Maximum LTV 75% of property value.

3.3 Interest Rates for SBI Home Loans (2025-2026):
- CIBIL 800+: EBLR + 0.00% = effective ~8.50% p.a.
- CIBIL 750-799: EBLR + 0.05% = effective ~8.55% p.a.
- CIBIL 700-749: EBLR + 0.10% = effective ~8.60% p.a.
- CIBIL 650-699: EBLR + 0.25% to 0.50% = effective ~8.75% to 9.00% p.a.
- Women Borrowers: Additional 0.05% concession on interest rate.
- NRI Home Loans: Subject to FEMA regulations; processing via designated NRI branches.

3.4 SBI Maxgain (Overdraft Home Loan):
- Operates as an overdraft account linked to home loan. Excess funds deposited reduce interest calculated on daily outstanding balance.
- Minimum Loan Amount: ₹20,00,000 (₹20 lakhs).
- Eligible for CIBIL 700 and above only.

3.5 Home Loan Documentation Checklist:
- Salaried: PAN, Aadhaar, 3 months salary slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months bank statements.
- Self-Employed: PAN, Aadhaar, ITR with computation (last 3 years), Audited Balance Sheet & P&L, GST Returns (12 months), 12 months current account statements.
- Property Documents: Sale deed / Agreement for Sale, Approved Building Plan, NOC from Society/Builder, Encumbrance Certificate.

CHAPTER 4: SBI PERSONAL LOAN PRODUCTS (XPRESS CREDIT & PENSION LOAN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 SBI Xpress Credit Eligibility & Income Requirements:
- Target Segment: Permanent employees of Central/State Govt, PSUs, Educational Institutions, and Corporate Companies (MNCs / Tier A listed entities) with salary account at SBI.
- Minimum Net Monthly Income (NMI) by City Tier:
  a) Chennai / Tier 1 Metro: Minimum NMI ₹24,000 per month (Gross approx ₹30,000).
  b) Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem (Tier 2): Minimum NMI ₹18,000 per month.
  c) Vellore, Rural TN (Tier 3): Minimum NMI ₹15,000 per month.
- Work Experience: Minimum 1 year total employment, minimum 6 months in current employer.
- Maximum Loan Amount: 24 times NMI, capped at ₹30,00,000 (₹30 lakhs).
- Maximum Tenure: 72 months (6 years).
- Interest Rate: From 10.90% to 15.65% p.a. depending on CIBIL score and employer category.

4.2 SBI Xpress Credit - NTC (New to Credit) Borrowers:
- Permitted for NTC customers earning above ₹30,000 NMI in Chennai (₹24,000 in Tier 2, ₹20,000 in Tier 3).
- Strictly limited to ₹5,00,000 maximum loan amount.
- Maximum FOIR 40% for NTC Personal Loan applicants.

4.3 SBI Personal Loan for Non-SBI Salary Customers (Insta Loan):
- Minimum CIBIL Score: 700 for non-SBI salary account holders.
- Minimum Income: ₹30,000 NMI for Tier 1, ₹25,000 for Tier 2, ₹20,000 for Tier 3.
- Maximum Loan: 10 times NMI, capped at ₹10,00,000.

4.4 SBI Pension Loan:
- Target: SBI-paying pensioners, including Central/State Govt pensioners and Defense pensioners.
- Maximum Loan: 18 months pension amount, capped at ₹14,00,000.
- Age Limit: Pensioner must not exceed 76 years at loan maturity.

4.5 Pre-closure & Foreclosure Charges:
- Zero foreclosure charges for SBI Salary Package account holders after 6 EMIs.
- Standard pre-closure fee of 3% on outstanding principal for non-package accounts.

CHAPTER 5: SBI SCHOLAR & HIGHER EDUCATION LOAN POLICY (STUDENT CREDIT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Institutional Tiering & Collateral Limits:
- List A Premier Institutes (IITs, IIMs, IISc, ISB, NLUs, AIIMS): Up to ₹40,00,000 WITHOUT COLLATERAL.
- List B Institutes (NITs, Premier State Universities, NIFT, NID): Up to ₹20,00,000 WITHOUT COLLATERAL.
- List C Institutes (State Recognized Colleges, private deemed universities): Up to ₹7,50,000 WITHOUT COLLATERAL; Above ₹7,50,000 requires 100% tangible collateral (residential property or FD).
- Overseas Studies (SBI Global Ed-Vantage): Up to ₹1,50,00,000 with 100% tangible security (property/FD).
- Vocational/Skill Development Courses: Up to ₹1,50,000 without collateral under PM Vidyalaxmi scheme.

5.2 Interest Rates for Education Loans:
- Loans up to ₹7.5 lakhs: Base rate approximately 10.00% p.a. (subject to RBI MCLR revisions).
- Loans above ₹7.5 lakhs: Base rate approximately 10.50% to 11.00% p.a.
- Women students receive 0.50% concession on interest rate.
- Moratorium Period: Course Duration + 12 months (or 6 months after securing employment, whichever is earlier). Simple interest accrues during moratorium; servicing optional.

5.3 Central Sector Interest Subsidy (CSIS) & PM Vidyalaxmi:
- Full interest subsidy during entire moratorium for EWS families (annual income up to ₹4,50,000).
- PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme: 75% credit guarantee for unsecured education loans up to ₹10 lakhs.
- For families with income between ₹4.5 lakhs and ₹8 lakhs: 3% interest subvention for the first 4 years.

5.4 Documentation for Education Loans:
- Student: Aadhaar, PAN, Admission Letter from institution, Fee schedule, 2 passport photos.
- Co-Borrower (Parent/Guardian): PAN, Aadhaar, Income proof (ITR/Form 16/Salary Slips), 6 months bank statement.
- For loans above ₹7.5 lakhs: Property documents or FD receipts as collateral.

CHAPTER 6: SBI KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC) & AGRICULTURAL LOANS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Scope & Scale of Finance:
- Scale of Finance (SOF) determined by District Level Technical Committee (DLTC) per acre for Paddy, Sugarcane, Cotton, Groundnut, Maize, Pulses, Banana, Onion.
- Collateral-Free Limit: Standard ₹1,60,000; Extended to ₹2,00,000 for Tamil Nadu farmers with online Chitta/Adangal land verification.
- KCC Renewal/Enhancement: Annual renewal based on crop cycle and repayment record.

6.2 Interest Subvention & Incentive Structure:
- Base Interest Rate: 7.00% p.a. for short-term crop loans up to ₹3,00,000.
- Government 2% Interest Subvention reduces base rate to 5.00% p.a.
- Additional 3% Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI) reduces effective rate to 4.00% p.a. for farmers repaying on time.
- ATM-enabled RuPay KCC cards issued for crop loan withdrawals at any ATM.

6.3 KCC Documentation:
- PAN or Form 60, Aadhaar, Chitta/Adangal (Tamil Nadu land record), VAO crop cultivation certificate, 2 photographs, Proof of landholding.

CHAPTER 7: SBI MUDRA & MSME BUSINESS LOANS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana at SBI:
- Shishu: Up to ₹50,000 via YONO Instant Mudra (e-Mudra disbursal within 5 minutes for existing savings customers with 6 months account history).
- Kishore: ₹50,001 to ₹5,00,000 (business plan required, 2 years vintage).
- Tarun: ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000 (audited financials required, 3 years vintage).
- Tarun Plus: ₹10,00,001 to ₹20,00,000 (collateral/third-party guarantee required).
- CGTMSE Coverage: Collateral-free up to ₹5,00,00,000 for eligible MSME manufacturing/service units.

7.2 SBI SME Credit Loan (Business Installment Loan):
- Maximum Loan: ₹5,00,00,000 for manufacturing; ₹2,00,00,000 for services/retail.
- Interest Rate: From 8.90% p.a. for CIBIL 750+ businesses; up to 13.00% p.a. for lower-rated units.
- Business Vintage Required: Minimum 3 years with audited profit and positive net worth.

CHAPTER 8: CUSTOMER RIGHTS & GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL AT SBI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 Key Fact Statement & APR:
- Mandatory KFS provided for all retail loans with complete Annual Percentage Rate (APR) disclosure.
- Cooling-off period: 3 days for Personal Loans, 7 days for Housing Loans.

8.2 Complaint Resolution Steps:
- Step 1: Contact Branch Manager.
- Step 2: Toll Free 1800 1234 / 1800 2100 (24x7).
- Step 3: Principal Nodal Officer at SBI LHO Chennai.
- Step 4: RBI Integrated Ombudsman (https://cms.rbi.org.in).

CHAPTER 9: EXHAUSTIVE DOCUMENTATION CHECKLIST FOR SBI LOANS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Salaried (Home Loan): PAN, Aadhaar, 3 months salary slips, Form 16 (2 years), 6 months salary account bank statement, Property documents.
- Salaried (Personal Loan): PAN, Aadhaar, 3 months salary slips, 3 months bank statement.
- Self-Employed (Any Loan): PAN, Aadhaar, ITR with computation (3 years), Audited Balance Sheet & P&L, GST Returns (12 months), 12 months current account statements.
- KCC/Agri: PAN or Form 60, Aadhaar, Chitta/Adangal land proof, VAO crop cultivation certificate, 2 photos.
- Education Loan: PAN, Aadhaar, Admission Letter, Fee Structure, Co-borrower income proof, Collateral documents if applicable.

CHAPTER 10: POLICY DEVIATIONS, HARD RULES & RELATIONSHIP CONCESSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 Hard Rejection Criteria (No Deviation Permitted):
- CIBIL score below 650 (non-salary account, non-CSP).
- FOIR exceeding 65% (or enhanced limit if applicable).
- Age below 18 or above 70 at Home Loan maturity.
- NPA (Non-Performing Asset) tag or 90+ Days Past Due (DPD) in last 24 months.
- Loan amount exceeding permissible LTV ratio.

10.2 Concessions & Leniency:
- SBI Corporate Salary Package (CSP): 50 points CIBIL margin leniency (min 650), 5% FOIR relaxation, 0.05% interest concession, zero processing fees.
- Women Home Loan Borrowers: 0.05% interest concession on all SBI Home Loan variants.
- Tier A MNC Employer: Additional 5% FOIR relaxation; eligible for FOIR up to 75% with CPC approval.
- Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) beneficiaries: Interest subsidy up to ₹2,67,280 for EWS/LIG category under CLSS.
