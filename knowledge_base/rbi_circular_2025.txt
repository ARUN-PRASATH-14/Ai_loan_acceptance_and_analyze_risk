Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Circular - Guidelines on Home Loan Eligibility (2025)

1. Scope and Applicability
This circular applies to all scheduled commercial banks (SCBs), including State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Indian Overseas Bank (IOB), and Canara Bank.

2. Loan to Value (LTV) Ratios
To ensure systemic stability, the RBI mandates the following maximum LTV ratios for housing loans:
- For loan amounts up to ₹30 lakh: Maximum LTV is 90%.
- For loan amounts between ₹30 lakh and ₹75 lakh: Maximum LTV is 80%.
- For loan amounts above ₹75 lakh: Maximum LTV is 75%.
Banks must strictly adhere to these limits.

3. Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR)
While banks have internal discretion, the RBI advises that the FOIR should generally not exceed 65% for any borrower to prevent over-leveraging.
- For first-time borrowers (New to Credit / No Credit History), banks are advised to implement a stricter FOIR cap, typically not exceeding 50%.
- If a co-applicant is added to the loan, their verifiable income may be added to the primary applicant's income for the purpose of FOIR calculation.

4. Credit Score Guidelines
The minimum acceptable credit score (e.g., CIBIL) is subject to individual bank policies:
- SBI typically requires a minimum CIBIL score of 700 for standard housing loans.
- HDFC and ICICI require a minimum CIBIL score of 650.
- Canara Bank requires a minimum CIBIL score of 600.
Applicants with no credit history (CIBIL 0) may still be considered if they demonstrate stable income, but they are subject to stricter FOIR and income minimums.

5. Prepayment Penalties
Banks are prohibited from charging foreclosure charges or prepayment penalties on any floating rate term loans sanctioned to individual borrowers for purposes other than business.
